Immigrant Experience & Needs Assessment

A questionnaire to understand the lived experience, challenges, and unmet needs of people navigating immigration processes — and to guide what Terra builds next. Estimated completion time: 20–25 minutes.

Purpose. Terra is building tools to help immigrants navigate complex, high-stakes immigration processes. This questionnaire is designed to help us understand your experience in your own words and through structured questions — what has been hardest, what support has helped, and what a good solution would actually need to do. There are no right or wrong answers; we are interested in your honest experience.

Confidentiality. Your participation is voluntary, and you may skip any question or stop at any time without consequence. Your responses are not connected to any active immigration case, application, or government agency, and will not affect any case outcome. If your name or contact information is collected (Section E, optional only), it will be stored separately from your responses and used only if you ask to be contacted.

How to answer. Section C asks you to rate statements on a 5-point scale from Strongly Disagree to Strongly Agree — choose the option that best reflects how true each statement is for you. Other sections ask you to select an option or write a short answer in your own words. Answer based on your own experience, even if it differs from what you think is "typical."

Section A

Respondent & Case Background

These questions help us understand who is answering, so we can see how experiences and needs differ across case types, regions, and stages — not to identify you personally. Every question here is optional.

Section B

In Your Own Words

Please answer as openly as you'd like — there is no length requirement, and every question here is optional. These responses will be read closely and grouped into themes; specific quotes may be used (with identifying details removed) to help our team understand what to build.

Section C

Your Experience — Rate Each Statement

For each statement, choose the option that best reflects how true it is for you right now. Every statement is optional — skip any that don't apply. Items marked (R) are reverse-worded on purpose — please still answer based on how much you agree with the statement exactly as written.

Section D

Rating Potential Solutions

For each idea below, rate how useful it would be to you personally, from 1 (Not useful) to 5 (Very useful). Every item here is optional.

Section E

Optional Follow-Up

Everything below is optional. Leave blank to remain fully anonymous.
